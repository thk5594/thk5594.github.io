I am a postdoctoral researcher at HKUST Business School, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with [Prof. Dongwook Shin](https://dwshin.people.ust.hk/).

My research focuses on stochastic simulation and sequential decision-making under uncertainty, with applications in operations research and operations management. My interests include Monte Carlo methods, data-driven stochastic modeling, and simulation analytics.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences from Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) in August 2021, advised by [Prof. Kyoung-Kuk Kim](https://sites.google.com/site/catenoid/home).
